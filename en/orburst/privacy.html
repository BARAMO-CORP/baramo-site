Orburst – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Orburst is a mobile game published by Baramo ("we"), a French company whose details are given in the legal notice. This page explains what data is processed when you play, and why.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. Orburst has no account, no sign-in and no online server of its own. Your progress, stars, scores and settings are saved only on your device. They are erased if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

Orburst is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To display ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software included in the game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country, city) can be derived;
  • technical information about your device (model, system version, language) and diagnostics;
  • your interactions with the ads (ads shown, taps).

This data is sent to Google and handled under Google's own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time with the "Privacy" button in the game's settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • On iOS, the game asks for your permission before any tracking across apps.

Purchases

If you buy the option that removes ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Orburst is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure and objection. As we hold no data about you, these rights are exercised with Google for advertising data. You can also write to us, and lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr), if you believe your rights are not respected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
